Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence (AI): AI is the simulation of human intelligence in machines, enabling them to perform tasks like problem-solving, decision-making, and learning. It encompasses various subfields, including machine learning, deep learning, and natural language processing. AI can be rule-based (symbolic AI) or data-driven (machine learning-based). Its applications range from automation to robotics and intelligent virtual assistants.
Machine Learning (ML): ML is a subset of AI that enables computers to learn patterns from data and make predictions or decisions without explicit programming. It uses algorithms such as supervised, unsupervised, and reinforcement learning to improve performance over time. ML powers applications like recommendation systems, fraud detection, and predictive analytics. The effectiveness of ML models depends on the quality and quantity of data used for training.
Deep Learning (DL): DL is a specialized branch of machine learning that uses artificial neural networks with multiple layers (deep networks) to process complex data. It excels in tasks like image recognition, speech processing, and natural language understanding due to its ability to learn hierarchical features. DL relies on massive datasets and high computational power, often utilizing GPUs for efficient training. Popular architectures include convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and recurrent neural networks (RNNs).
Natural Language Processing (NLP): NLP is a field of AI that focuses on enabling machines to understand, interpret, and generate human language. It combines computational linguistics with machine learning to process text and speech for tasks like translation, sentiment analysis, and chatbots. NLP techniques include tokenization, named entity recognition, and syntactic parsing. Applications include virtual assistants, search engines, and automated text summarization.
